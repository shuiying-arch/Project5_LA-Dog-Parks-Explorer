Dog Parks in Los Angeles County

About This Map

LA Dog Parks Explorer helps dog owners find parks with dog park facilities across Los Angeles County. Users can explore park locations, search by park name, and filter parks with restrooms.

What's New in Project 5?

  • Search dog parks by name.
  • Filter parks with restroom facilities.
  • Display the number of matching parks.
  • Improved mobile-friendly layout.
